Cloudflare kicks off Agents Week

Cloudflare Blog, published 2026-04-12

The post sets up a broader week of agent infrastructure announcements and positions Cloudflare around edge-native orchestration for long-running agents.

NVIDIA Developer Blog, published 2026-04-12 01:02 UTC

The post also ties the model to NVIDIA’s NemoClaw reference stack plus vLLM and SGLang optimizations for large-scale inference.
